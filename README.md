# New Wheels Business Performance Analysis Using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance, customer satisfaction, shipping efficiency, and revenue trends for New Wheels, a vehicle resale company experiencing declining sales and increasing customer concerns.

Using SQL and MySQL, I explored customer feedback data, order activity, shipping timelines, and sales performance to identify key business challenges and provide data-driven recommendations for improvement.

## Tools & Skills Used

- SQL
- MySQL
- Joins
- Aggregate Functions
- Window Functions
- Subqueries
- Data Analysis
- Business Intelligence
- Problem Solving

## Business Questions Addressed

1. Distribution of customers across states
2. Average customer ratings by quarter
3. Most preferred vehicle manufacturers by state
4. Trend of customer feedback over time
5. Quarterly sales and revenue performance
6. Shipping time analysis
7. Impact of discounts and payment methods

## Key Findings

- Customer satisfaction declined throughout the year.
- Negative customer feedback increased over time.
- Shipping times increased significantly across quarters.
- Revenue and order volume declined from Q1 to Q4.
- Certain manufacturers demonstrated stronger regional customer preference.

## Recommendations

- Improve shipping and fulfillment processes.
- Address customer service concerns driving negative feedback.
- Develop retention strategies for existing customers.
- Monitor regional manufacturer performance for targeted marketing.
